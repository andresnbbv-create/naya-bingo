OFFICIAL RULES OF PLAY

🎯 The Goal
Complete 5 activities in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — to earn a special thank-you gift from Naya Homes!

🎟️ Getting Started

  1. Choose your region (Puerto Vallarta or Nuevo Vallarta)
  2. Pick your check-in date
  3. Your custom weekly card loads — 25 unique experiences!

🎲 How to Play

Tap a tile → Go do the activity → Take a photo → Upload it → Mark complete. Get 5 in a row to claim your gift!

🎁 Claiming Your Reward

Once you complete a line of 5, tap "Claim My Gift" and enter your name + reservation. You'll get a unique Naya Token (like PALM-OCEAN-427). Show it to a Naya Homes team member to redeem.

📌 Save your token! Screenshot or write it down — you'll need it to claim your gift.
